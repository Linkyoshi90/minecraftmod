Rodents

Two rodent monster girls: the capybara, calmest animal alive, and Ratatoskr, the squirrel who runs up and down the world tree carrying messages. Small, busy, and good company.

Favourite food: Seeds, nuts and greens; the capybara loves melon

The central similarity

Gnawers and gatherers.

Rodents gather: the capybara gathers friends around her, Ratatoskr gathers seeds and saplings (and forgets half of them).

  • CalmNo predator hunts near a capybara.
  • CachesRatatoskr's forgotten caches grow into trees.
  • MessagesRatatoskr carries items between friends.
  • CompanyIdle girls like to sit beside a capybara.

Relationships (shared by every family)

The same relationship core runs through all MonsterGirls families, from the harpies on. This volume only changes how a rodent builds trust, what she gives back, and how she bears young.

AffectionEach monster girl remembers every player separately. Feeding, petting and her family's own bond action raise it; hurting her lowers it. /monstergirls bonds lists everyone nearby.
SitRight-click any monster girl with a plain stick to make her sit; again to let her get up.
Sharing a bedAt high friendship (25) she may join you in bed: once a night she decides, one in three at 25, certain at 100. Sleep through the night and she is expecting.
ExpectingA pregnancy lasts one in-game day. Her belly rounds out as it goes on (the model shows it).
Born devotedHer young are born devoted to the player who fathered them (full affection) and never share that player's bed.
PresentsAt high friendship every family brings presents of its own kind.

Behaviour & gameplay (shared by the family)

CalmWolves, foxes, cats and ocelots hunt nothing within 8 blocks of a capybara; idle girls nearby sit beside her.
Scatter-hoardingRatatoskr picks up seeds and saplings and plants the ones she forgets.
MessengerHand Ratatoskr an item: she carries it to the nearest other player she is fond of (now and then swapped as a prank).

Capybara (about 1.6 blocks)

TemperamentUnflappable

Ecology & behaviourHydrochoerus hydrochaeris (Rodentia: Caviidae), the largest rodent alive, famous for letting every other animal sit on or beside her.

Signature: CalmNo wolf, fox, cat or ocelot hunts within 8 blocks of her. Idle girls nearby sit down beside her. She soaks in water with a yuzu on her head.

Player interactionMelon, sugar cane and apples. Just sit with her.
