The central similarity

Roll up.

Frightened, she rolls up into a ball, and nothing gets through. Befriended, she rolls into whatever goes for you.

  • BallNo damage while rolled up.
  • Rolling chargeBefriended, she rolls into monsters.
  • ScutesShe sheds armadillo scutes as presents.
  • KinVanilla armadillos.

Relationships (shared by every family)

The same relationship core runs through all MonsterGirls families, from the harpies on. This volume only changes how a armoured builds trust, what she gives back, and how she bears young.

AffectionEach monster girl remembers every player separately. Feeding, petting and her family's own bond action raise it; hurting her lowers it. /monstergirls bonds lists everyone nearby.
SitRight-click any monster girl with a plain stick to make her sit; again to let her get up.
Sharing a bedAt high friendship (25) she may join you in bed: once a night she decides, one in three at 25, certain at 100. Sleep through the night and she is expecting.
ExpectingA pregnancy lasts one in-game day. Her belly rounds out as it goes on (the model shows it).
Born devotedHer young are born devoted to the player who fathered them (full affection) and never share that player's bed.
PresentsAt high friendship every family brings presents of its own kind.

Rolling chargeBefriended, she curls up and rolls into a monster near her friend, knocking it back.

TemperamentTimid, then very tough

Ecology & behaviourArmadillos (Cingulata: Dasypodidae): banded armour of bony scutes. The three-banded one rolls into a perfect ball.

Signature: Roll upA hit or a sprinting stranger rolls her into a ball: she takes no damage. Befriended, she rolls into monsters near her friend. The ranger variant wears a cowboy hat and scarf, a rolling frontier guard.

TemperamentSlow, serene

Ecology & behaviourA Harmontide sloth, a cousin of the armadillos, so slow that moss and flowers grow in her fur.
