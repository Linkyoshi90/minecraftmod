Mixed Slime (about 1.6 blocks)

TemperamentTwo moods at once

Ecology & behaviourTwo slime girls of different kinds who trust the same player can flow together into one marbled slime. She is both of them: a killing blow does not split her into halves, it separates her back into the two she was.

Signature: Two in oneOne slime carrying both girls, their bonds and their belongings. Never lost: hurt badly, she comes apart into the two again.

Player interactionHold a slime ball and right-click one of two slimes (of different kinds, both at high friendship with you) standing together. Sneak and do it again to separate them.
