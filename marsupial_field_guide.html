Relationships (shared by every family)

The same relationship core runs through all Harmontide families, from the harpies on. This volume only changes how a marsupial builds trust, what she gives back, and how she bears young.

AffectionEach monster girl remembers every player separately. Feeding, petting and her family's own bond action raise it; hurting her lowers it. /harmontide bonds lists everyone nearby.
SitRight-click any monster girl with a plain stick to make her sit; again to let her get up.
Sharing a bedAt high friendship (25) she may join you in bed: once a night she decides, one in three at 25, certain at 100. Sleep through the night and she is expecting.
ExpectingA pregnancy lasts one in-game day. Her belly rounds out as it goes on (the model shows it).
Born devotedHer young are born devoted to the player who fathered them (full affection) and never share that player's bed.
PresentsAt high friendship every family brings presents of its own kind.

Koala (about 1.4 blocks)

TemperamentSleepy

Ecology & behaviourPhascolarctos cinereus (Diprotodontia: Phascolarctidae), who eats only leaves and sleeps most of the day.

Signature: AsleepAwake only at dawn and dusk; the rest of the time she sleeps clinging to a tree trunk. Her joey rides on her back. The rare drop bear drops onto whoever walks under her tree (a hug, once she likes you).
