Crustaceans

Beach-combing crab girls with a hard shell and harder claws. They mind their own business, sideways, until someone hits them.

Favourite food: Fish and kelp

The central similarity

Armour on the outside.

A crab carries her skeleton outside: a carapace on her back and two big claws. The hermit carries a borrowed shell instead.

  • SidewaysThey walk sideways.
  • PinchHit one and she pinches back.
  • ShellsThe hermit crab swaps shells: give her a nautilus or shulker shell.
  • BeachesSand, shores and warm shallows.

Relationships (shared by every family)

The same relationship core runs through all MonsterGirls families, from the harpies on. This volume only changes how a crustacean builds trust, what she gives back, and how she bears young.

AffectionEach monster girl remembers every player separately. Feeding, petting and her family's own bond action raise it; hurting her lowers it. /monstergirls bonds lists everyone nearby.
SitRight-click any monster girl with a plain stick to make her sit; again to let her get up.
Sharing a bedAt high friendship (25) she may join you in bed: once a night she decides, one in three at 25, certain at 100. Sleep through the night and she is expecting.
ExpectingA pregnancy lasts one in-game day. Her belly rounds out as it goes on (the model shows it).
Born devotedHer young are born devoted to the player who fathered them (full affection) and never share that player's bed.
PresentsAt high friendship every family brings presents of its own kind.

Crab (about 1.5 blocks)

TemperamentBusy, defensive

Ecology & behaviourTrue crabs (Decapoda: Brachyura): a hard carapace, two claws, and a sideways walk.

Signature: PinchNeutral: she pinches whoever hits her first (Slowness). The decorator variant dresses her shell in kelp and coral, like the spider crab's relatives.

Player interactionFish and kelp. Do not hit her.
