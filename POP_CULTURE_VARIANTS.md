# Pop-culture homage variants

Ideas for homage variants: a rare look of an existing monster girl that nods to a character from games (and a few
other media). They follow the rules of the Gregory Horror Show homages (HARMONTIDE_CONCEPTS.md, the "Homage variants"
of 2026-10-25): a generic variant name, no logos or lettering, the character's colours and one or two signature props,
rolled rarely in the wild (`AbstractMonsterGirl.HOMAGES`). Variant names must be unique across all species, because
the homage set is keyed by name.

**Fit** is how naturally the character sits on that girl (the same animal, the same rig, the props we can model).
✅ = in the game (2026-10-28: four with the journal, eight with the nineteenth wave).

## Possible but weaker

- Lamia (any) as Solid Snake (Metal Gear): headband, sneaking suit, a cardboard box. Funny, but a lamia has no legs to crouch with.
- Elf archer as Link (Zelda): the green cap and tunic fit, but the character is male. A Zelda look (princess dress) fits the high elf better.
- Ent as Groot (Marvel): not a game.
- Yuki-onna as Frosslass (Pokémon): already close to the yuki-onna's own look.
- Dullahan as Celty (Durarara!!): anime, and a near match for the base design already.
- Medusa as Medusa from Kid Icarus: very close to the base design, little to add.

## Notes for building them

- The ★★★ ones need only a palette and at most one prop that the family generator already has (a hat, a tail, a crest).
  The rest want one new prop each (goggles, a traffic cone, a teacup).
- Name them after what the character is, not who: `jewel_thief`, not `rouge`.
- Each new homage variant goes at the end of the species' variant list (gen script and concept entry, kept in sync),
  and its name into `AbstractMonsterGirl.HOMAGES`.
