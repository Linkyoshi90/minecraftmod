The Tree of Life

519 kinds on 34 branches: 27 real clades and 7 groups of legend that sit outside the tree. A girl's body (her form, the word in her id) decides how she moves, nests and behaves; her branch decides her milk, the name on her cheese, her spawn-rate setting and the journal's tree of life. Each name links to her card in the guide for her form.

How to read it

  • BranchWhere she really sits, from class down to family. The body animal decides; horns, wings and tails don't.
  • GuestHer true kin are too few here (fewer than four), so she lives with her nearest relatives. Her true place is still given.
  • Judgement callA placement that could reasonably go elsewhere (TAXONOMY_PLAN.md lists the reasons).
  • MilkWhat a bucket of that branch's milk does for whoever drinks it. Plants, fungi and constructs give none.
