The central similarity

Made, not born.

A construct is shaped, not born: she has no young, and when she falls she breaks apart into clay.

  • ToughestMore health than an iron golem.
  • GuardianShe fights monsters near villagers.
  • KinIron golems follow her.
  • BreaksShe breaks into pieces when she falls.

Relationships (shared by every family)

The same relationship core runs through all MonsterGirls families, from the harpies on. This volume only changes how a construct builds trust, what she gives back, and how she bears young.

AffectionEach monster girl remembers every player separately. Feeding, petting and her family's own bond action raise it; hurting her lowers it. /monstergirls bonds lists everyone nearby.
SitRight-click any monster girl with a plain stick to make her sit; again to let her get up.
Sharing a bedAt high friendship (25) she may join you in bed: once a night she decides, one in three at 25, certain at 100. Sleep through the night and she is expecting.
ExpectingA pregnancy lasts one in-game day. Her belly rounds out as it goes on (the model shows it).
Born devotedHer young are born devoted to the player who fathered them (full affection) and never share that player's bed.
PresentsAt high friendship every family brings presents of its own kind.

In the game

Built (2026-10-19)

  • Golem: 120 health (more than an iron golem), heavy blows
  • Fights monsters near villagers (never creepers), and near her devoted friend
  • Iron golems are her kin
  • Made, not born: no bed, no young; clay heals her
  • Breaks into pieces when she falls
  • Talosling: redstone dust winds her (5 minutes each, 20 at most); wound up, she guards her friend; run down, she sits still until wound again
  • Gargoyle: stone by day (nothing hurts her), awake at night: she flies and fights monsters near her friend
