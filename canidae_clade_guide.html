Canids (wolves, dogs & foxes)

Wolves, dogs, jackals and every fox, the kitsune included: one family of long-legged runners, with the hellhounds of legend. Foxes were a family of their own in the old sorting; here they rejoin their cousins, and the tanuki, a raccoon dog, comes with them.
