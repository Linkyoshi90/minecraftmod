Theropods

Hunters who walk like the old museum sketches of a T. rex: leaning forward over a long tail held out for balance, with a grin full of little sharp teeth. Human in every other way.

The central similarity

Lean in, tail out.

A theropod girl stands hunched forward, her heavy tail held level behind her to balance. Under it she is human: human legs, arms, skin and face. Each kind keeps a little of her dinosaur: a sail, claws, feathers.

  • PostureHunched forward, tail out behind for balance.
  • TeethA grin full of small pointed teeth.
  • MeatThe family food: raw meat, and eggs.
  • CousinsLike the lizardfolk they bask and lay leathery eggs.

Relationships (shared by every family)

The same relationship core runs through all MonsterGirls families, from the harpies on. This volume only changes how a theropod builds trust, what she gives back, and how she bears young.

AffectionEach monster girl remembers every player separately. Feeding, petting and her family's own bond action raise it; hurting her lowers it. /monstergirls bonds lists everyone nearby.
SitRight-click any monster girl with a plain stick to make her sit; again to let her get up.
Sharing a bedAt high friendship (25) she may join you in bed: once a night she decides, one in three at 25, certain at 100. Sleep through the night and she is expecting.
ExpectingA pregnancy lasts one in-game day. Her belly rounds out as it goes on (the model shows it).
Born devotedHer young are born devoted to the player who fathered them (full affection) and never share that player's bed.
PresentsAt high friendship every family brings presents of its own kind.

Mating & young

FormA leathery clutch. Young2-4 hatchlings, grown in 5 days. WhereWarm sand or dirt. Hatchlings use the adult model at 45%.

1. CourtshipAt high friendship she may share your bed.
2. EggsShe lays a leathery clutch of two to four eggs.
3. HatchingAfter a day the hatchlings break out, each a random colour of her species.
4. GrownGrown in five days.

In the game

Built (2026-10-19)

  • Hunched forward over a long balancing tail, human legs, arms and face, a sharp-toothed grin (the spinosaur alone has a snout)
  • Cousins of the lizardfolk: cold-blooded, basking, scales are armour, leathery clutches
  • Raw meat and eggs
  • Fernstalker (moved from the lizardfolk): befriended, she pounces on a monster near her friend
  • Spinosaur: befriended, her friend near her in water swims like a dolphin (Dolphin's Grace)
  • Australovenator: befriended, when her friend strikes a mob she leaps on it too (never a pet)

Not yet

  • More theropods (a T. rex, a dilophosaurus, a velociraptor)

Design decisions log

Decided (2026-10-19)

  • Built as their own family on 2026-10-29: the fernstalker moved over from the lizardfolk, the spinosaur and the australovenator came with it (planned for the twenty-second wave).
