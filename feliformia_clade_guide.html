Feliforms (cats & kin)

The cat-shaped half of the carnivores: true cats, the sphinxes and lamassu of legend, and the kin that don't look it. The hyena runs in a dog's body and the mongoose and meerkat in a rodent's, but all three sit on the cat side of the split. The pangolin lives here as a guest.
